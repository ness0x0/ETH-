# Executive investment memo — Ethereum NFTs vs BTC

_Prepared 2026-10-13 for a BTC-primary investor considering $7–8k in Ethereum NFTs (and holding one Azuki).
Market: BTC $83,350 · ETH $2,508.69 · ETH/BTC 0.0301 (Binance daily close). Supporting work: the
[shortlist](shortlist.md), [scenario model](scenario_model.md), [historical analysis](historical_analysis.md),
[on-chain analysis](onchain_collector_analysis.md), [liquidity](liquidity.md), [critic review](critic_review.md) and
[response](critic_response.md), [uncertainties](uncertainties.md), and the [dossiers](../dossiers/)._

## Bottom line

1. **Don't buy Ethereum NFTs now.** None of the 47 collections we screened offers mispriced survivability. That would
   mean limited downside and large upside against BTC. The ones that look culturally durable have the worst return
   evidence. The ones with the best return evidence depend on a company or founder to manufacture catalysts.
2. **If your reason for considering NFTs is the "ETH and on-chain speculation come back" scenario, ETH itself is the
   better instrument.**
   - ETH beats BTC in expectation at ~14% weight on the strong/mania scenarios. The best NFTs need 18–22% *with*
     strict exit discipline (Chromie Squiggle 55%). Held without discipline, all but V1 and CryptoDickbutts never
     break even.
   - In the weak scenario ETH loses ~20% vs BTC. The NFTs lose 51–75%.
   - NFTs add value only in a speculative mania, and only for a holder who sells into it.
3. **Your Azuki: exit patiently, not now.** It fails the mandate: it depends on a company whose IP strategy bypasses
   the NFT, and it beat BTC in 1–7% of historical 18-month windows. But today's bid (0.466 ETH) is depressed by
   Blur/Blend and BendDAO forced-selling ahead of the 2026-11-12 Blend deadline. List near the prevailing floor after
   that deadline clears, or on any rebound, and move the proceeds to BTC.
4. **If you still want a speculative "mania ticket,"** wait for the entry gates in the [shortlist](shortlist.md):
   - post-Blur liquidity is confirmed;
   - ETH/BTC is above its 30-week trend *and* NFT trading volume turns up;
   - your own weight on a strong recovery is ≥~25%.

   Then buy **one V1 CryptoPunk (~1.5 ETH, about half the budget) by bid**, and sell it under a pre-committed rule:
   once it is ≥1.5× vs BTC, sell on a 30% drawdown from its peak. Alternatives in order: Milady (a mandate exception),
   CryptoDickbutts (small and patient), and Chromie Squiggle only as a collectible you want to own.

## Addendum (2026-10-14): your cycle-timing framing — tested on the 2024–25 cycle

You asked a different question: assume BTC goes 2.16–2.52× to its top ($180–210k), buy NFTs near the cycle low, sell
into the euphoria. Does that beat BTC? The most recent analog is BTC's ~2.1× leg from the Sep-2024 low to the
Oct-2025 top ([full analysis](cycle_analysis.md); chart `reports/charts/cycle2024_low_to_high_vs_btc.png`).

- **In 2024–25, 33 of 44 collections (75%) beat BTC** when bought near the 2024 low, sold in the euphoric month and
  rotated into BTC. That held even though ETH/BTC slipped from 0.044 to ~0.04. Finalists: 7 of 8.
- **Applied to today,** using each collection's own 2024–25 swing:
  - ~77–91% odds of beating BTC and ~1-in-3 to 1-in-2 odds of doubling BTC's result, for ETH/BTC 0.025–0.035 at
    the next euphoria.
  - The harder full-cycle test (from the 2022 low) gives ~2 in 5.
- **Why that is an upper bound:**
  - It is one cycle, so its outcomes are correlated, not independent trials.
  - 59% of collections, including most PFPs, are now *below* their 2024 lows.
  - The token catalysts that drove the Dec-2024 winners are spent.
  - Survivors only.
  - It depends on actually selling into the euphoria and rotating into BTC; holding cash instead halves the win rate.
- **Net.** "No purchase" remains the unconditional answer. *If* you hold the BTC view with conviction, a capped,
  fully losable cycle-trade sleeve is supportable:
  - Names: V1 (1 item), Milady (2–3 items), and optionally a small CryptoDickbutts bid.
  - Entry: tranches starting after 2026-11-12, adding as evidence of a low accumulates.
  - Exit: sell into the next euphoria and rotate into BTC.

## Addendum 2 (2026-10-14): your bar is 4–10× in dollars while BTC does 2×

If you rotate the proceeds into BTC when you sell, 4× in dollars means the NFT must double *against BTC*; 10× means
5× against BTC. ([Section E of the cycle analysis](cycle_analysis.md#e-can-anything-do-410-in-dollars-while-btc-does-2-follow-ups-2026-10-14))

**The test.** Buy near the 2024 low and place a sell listing at the target in advance (no hindsight). In the 2024–25
analog:

| Sample | Hit 4× $ | Hit 10× $ | Lost to BTC |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 44 collections | 32% | 5% | 45% |
| **Small PFPs** (under $5M floor cap at the low) | **8 of 12** | 2 of 12 | 1 of 12 |
| $15M+ at the low | 21% | 0% | 64% |

- **Fills.** Thirteen of the 14 hits held the target for days to weeks, so a listed order would plausibly have filled.
- **The harsher 2022–25 test.** Small PFPs hit 4× in dollars 25–50% of the time; with a mistimed entry, most lost to
  BTC.
- **Today.** Applying the 2024–25 swings, small names hit 4× in dollars 53–82% of the time if ETH/BTC is 0.025–0.035 at
  the euphoria, and 24% at 0.020. These are upper bounds: one cycle, a dozen small PFPs, survivors (the most biased
  part), and a fresh low that hasn't arrived yet.

**This changes the shortlist for this objective.**
- V1, Milady, Pudgy and Squiggle peaked at 1.5–1.9× vs BTC in 2024–25: enough to beat BTC, not to reach 4× in
  dollars.
- The candidates are the 17 small, drawn-down PFPs in section E4; nine of them hit 2× vs BTC last cycle.

**How it has to be run.**
- **Entry.** Only after 2026-11-12, with evidence of a low.
- **Size.** As a loss you can take.
- **Exit, set on day one.** A sell listing at 2.2× entry in BTC terms, optionally half at ~5.5×, rotating fills
  into BTC.
- **Concentration.** One or two names for a real shot at 4× on the whole position; 3–5 names for a likely ~1.5× vs BTC
  with little chance of losing to BTC.

**10× in dollars stays a tail** (5–17%). New mints can't be projected.

**Buy-and-hold doesn't work even from the low.**
- Bought near the Oct-2023 low and held three years, none of 44 collections beat BTC.
- Bought near the 2024 low and held to BTC's Oct-2025 top, 41% beat BTC; held to today, 5% have.
- For legacy NFTs, the gain is in the sell, not the hold.

**Azuki.** Unchanged: exit patiently after 2026-11-12.

## Why

**1. The base rate is brutal and has not turned.**
- Since June 2022, the median screened collection lost 89% vs BTC. An equal-weight buy-and-hold basket lost 75%.
- Only 3 of 38 surviving collections beat BTC — Lil Pudgys, Pudgy and Milady — all company- or founder-driven. This
  universe was picked from survivors in 2026, so reality is worse.
- The sector fell vs BTC in every calendar year: 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 YTD.
- Universe-wide trading volume was $67M in Q3 2026, against $1.8–2.3B in early 2023 and 2024.

([historical analysis](historical_analysis.md); `reports/charts/sector_index_btc.png`, `sector_activity.png`)

**2. The upside is real but transient.**
- Each recent rally lifted the median collection 1.6–2.9× vs BTC within months.
- Within 3–6 months, 60–80% of the peak was gone. For the finalists, floors 365 days after the 2024/2025 peaks
  averaged 18–52% of the peak.
- Two of the three rally engines are gone: Blur airdrop farming (2023) and spent token airdrops (2024). The third
  (2025) was ETH's own rally.
- **Owning ETH NFTs is first a leveraged, noisy bet on ETH/BTC.** BTC-terms floors carry roughly 1× beta to ETH/BTC,
  plus a negative drift of about −33%/yr in ETH terms, with 85–200% annualised volatility.

**3. Catalysts don't create durable value for holders.** Across 151 independent events since 2022:
- Six token TGEs (PENGU, CULT, ANIME, BIRB, $gooch, LADYS) were followed by a median **−64% vs BTC at 90 days**
  (−47% vs the sector). TGEs have marked local tops.
- Brand and product news (Pudgy's retail push, Azuki's TCG and manga), museum gifts (MoMA, LACMA) and celebrity
  moments (Musk; Vitalik ×2) had no abnormal effect beyond a few weeks.
- The only lasting re-ratings came from structural, operator-driven events. Even those (Pudgy's Walmart run,
  Terraforms' on-chain libraries, V1's PunksMarket) are now fully reversed in BTC terms. The exception is the Moonbirds
  ownership change.

**4. Demand is thinner than it looks, and the venue is changing.**
- 68–82% of trailing-year PFP volume is dealer churn: bid-hit-and-relist arbitrage. Organic turnover is ~5–6
  sales/day for the big PFPs and 1–2.5/day for the art names.
- Pre-2024 holders were net sellers in all nine finalists.
- Pudgy and Azuki ran 52–58% of trailing-year sales through Blur/Blend, whose frontend closes on 2026-11-12.
- For a ~$7.5k position, the instant round-trip cost ranges from 7.5% (Pudgy) to 29% (Squiggle, which carries a 7.5%
  enforced royalty) and 41% (CryptoDickbutts).

## The Blur/Blend shutdown

- **What happened.** Floors of the Blend-financed PFPs fell hard into and after the 2026-10-12 announcement:
  - Pudgy 3.18 → 2.35 ETH, Azuki 0.63 → 0.49, Milady 0.93 → 0.74 (Oct 1–13).
  - On 13 Oct alone, 119 Pudgy, 68 Azuki and 25 Milady left Blend.
  - Art collections and CryptoPunks didn't react.
- **Temporary.** The forced-selling channel is small and shrinking. Lending escrow is 1.2–3.6% of supply for the PFPs
  (Blend 1–2.2%). It fell 35–47% over the past year, and Blend holdings are 67–87% below their peaks. One buyer absorbed 131 Pudgies in the sell-off. This pressure
  is plausibly temporary, and it is why we say not to sell the Azuki into it.
- **Structural.** Blur's venue and incentives provided over half of PFP liquidity. Their removal is a liquidity
  repricing, not just a dip. It is untested until after 2026-11-12, which is why gate G1 exists.
- **Not permanent impairment of collector demand.** There is no evidence the shutdown destroys demand. But nothing
  suggests the shutdown creates a bargain either.
- **Codex loan-book reconstruction:** not available at the time of writing. When it arrives, update
  `data/processed/onchain/lending_escrow_current.csv` and re-test G1 (see [uncertainties](uncertainties.md)).

## What would change this recommendation

- **Regime:**
  - ETH/BTC holds above its 30-week average; *and*
  - universe trading volume rises above ~$35M/month for two consecutive months, against $16–34M in 2026; *and*
  - organic (non-churn) sales rise.
- **A liquidity regime that survives Blur.** After 2026-11-12, funded bids sit within ~15% of floor for the target.
- **Collection-specific:**
  - V1: acknowledgement by NODE or a museum, or a burn or donation of the Yuga-linked 1,084 V1s.
  - Squiggle: royalty removed or reduced.
  - Pudgy: a genuine holder-value mechanism.
  - Milady: Remilia governance resolved and CULT supply burned or distributed.
- **A new incentive regime.** For example, an OpenSea SEA token that rewards NFT trading or staking (launch status
  unverified as of today). History says this creates a tradeable spike, not durable value.

## Costs and risks not in the numbers

- BTC→ETH conversion spread, gas and possible capital-gains tax on selling BTC.
- Wallet security. Signature-phishing drain patterns were observed in these collections; use a fresh hardware-signer
  wallet and never sign blind.
- Execution discipline. Rule B only works if followed mechanically.
- Venue and royalty policy changes.
