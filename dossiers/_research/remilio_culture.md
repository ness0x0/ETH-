- [F] **CULT presale.**
  - 250 "Community Entry" slots (0.25 ETH each) for Remilio holders, against 250 for Milady holders and 500 for any Remilia-snapshot wallet.
  - A holder curve was also offered (Remilia wiki `$CULT`).
  - The TGE seed airdrop was weighted about 80% on Remilia NFT holdings. **Per-NFT Remilio weight was never published** (UNVERIFIED).
- [OC] The CULT token has fallen from about $0.0057 at launch to $0.000117 (−98%; see the Milady dossier and `cult_price_samples.csv`). Any CULT kept by Remilio holders is now worth close to nothing.
- [F] **Beta to Milady.** Computed from NPF floors, 2023-01 to 2026-10, in `data/processed/npf_series`:
  - On roughly 30-day log returns, Remilio's beta to Milady is **1.32** with correlation **0.91** (n=46).
  - On 3-day bars, beta is 0.64 and correlation 0.36, which is dominated by floor noise.
  - The Remilio/Milady floor ratio ranged from 0.07 (Jan 2023) to 0.31 (Jul 2023). It is **0.16 today** (0.118/0.744).
- [I] **Asymmetric beta.** Remilio fell harder on Remilia-governance shocks (Sept 2023 −69% vs −35%) and on the CULT unwind (90d −77% vs −70% BTC). It captured less of celebrity spikes aimed at Milady specifically (Jan 2025: +3% vs +19% over 30d), but more of broad ecosystem rallies (Oct 2023 – Mar 2024: +212% vs +130%).

## 7. Competitive threats and displacement

[I] Remilio is threatened from several directions:
- Milady itself: when Milady is cheap (0.74 ETH), the "affordable Milady" niche shrinks.
- Any new Remilia collection or token.
- The many free or cheap Milady derivatives (Pixelady and others).
- Memecoins (CULT, LADYS) that offer Remilia exposure at any size.

## 8. "If the team vanished tomorrow, would collectors still want this in 2028?"

[I] **Unlikely at today's price, beyond a small sentimental core.** Remilio's identity is "Remilia's second collection". Without Remilia events, airdrops and Milady spillover, there is little to anchor it. On the investor's low-founder-dependence criterion, it is the weakest of the three.

The same list as Milady: CULT Manipulation Rounds 2 and 3, RemiliaChat, Remilia fashion and film, and celebrity PFP moments. Remilio would participate at about 1.3× Milady's beta. Base rate: these spikes retrace within 1–3 months.
