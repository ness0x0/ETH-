## 1. Identity and provenance

- [F] **Meme provenance.** "Dickbutt" was drawn by webcomic artist **K.C. Green** in *Horribleville* ("Tree. You've Been Good to Us", 2006-07-02). It became one of the oldest continuously used internet memes: Urban Dictionary 2007, BuzzFeed 2009 (Gooch Gazette, 2021-12-05; Decrypt, 2022-02-12).
- [F] **No verified K.C. Green licence or involvement.**
  - The NFT project says it originated in the CryptoPunks community as an homage to, and gift for, early Punk holders. It was "rumoured" to be tied to Larva Labs-era Punk holders (Gooch Gazette).
  - NFT Now (via search snippet; page blocked) reports that Green "denounced all association with his creation in 2021", and that the collection "isn't actually associated with the original creator". The project's own newsletter says Green "expressed a growing disdain for his creation".
  - IQ.wiki claims Green "created NFTs and established CryptoDickButts". That conflicts with every other source and has no primary evidence, so it is treated as an error.
  - **Licensing relationship with K.C. Green: none found (UNVERIFIED that any exists).**
- [F] **Creator/team.** Pseudonymous: "Daddy" per Gooch Gazette Issue 3, 2022-06-06. CDB shares a Solidity developer with Gremplin's CrypToadz. That is a shared contractor, and there is no evidence Gremplin created CDB.
  - **"Gooch"** is the in-lore island (Gooch Island) and the name of the DAO newsletter (*The Gooch Gazette*) and of the `$gooch` token. No source identifies a team member called "Gooch".
- [F] **Supply.**
  - OG Series 1 & 2: 161 NFTs (52 + 104 + reserves), March 2021.
  - [OC] **Series 3** (this contract): 5,200 minted from **2021-07-28 17:36 UTC** (block 12,915,737) to **2021-08-07 00:01 UTC** (block 12,974,523). Public price was **0.052 ETH**, with bulk discounts (e.g. 16 for 0.7488) and zero-value claim batches of 47–50.
  - Series 4: July/Aug 2023. Size and price UNVERIFIED.
- [F] **Licence.** The project started under a modified Nifty licence. In 2022 the community voted to go **CC0** (vote text quoted in Gooch Gazette, 2022-06-06; the 2022-03-18 tweet date comes from the CC0 wiki via search snippet).
  - [I] CC0 covers the CDB artwork. The underlying character is Green's, so what "IP" can be owned or sold is legally ambiguous.

## 3. Founder/team dependence and status as of Oct 2026

- [F] **The original team exited by design in Aug 2023** (the Series 4 "largest holder takes control" mechanism).
- [F] Since Nov 2025, control sits with a **community DAO ("Penus DAO") plus one whale-collector, Adam Weitsman**. Weitsman is a scrap-metal billionaire who says he has "never sold an NFT" (Decrypt, 2025-11-23).
  - No roadmap, product releases or 2026 announcements were found.
  - No newsletter posts after 2022 were found (not exhaustively checked).
- [I] **This makes CDB the closest thing in the set to a natural experiment of "the team vanished".**
  - After the founders left (Aug 2023), the collection lost about 84% in BTC terms over 14 months.
  - It then had a 2025 rebound that coincided with an ETH/BTC recovery and a meme-coin revival of the Dickbutt meme, and then faded.
  - So the collection **survived** without a team (2,131 owners, still trading). It did not **hold value** without one.
- [I] **New concentration risk.** Steering now depends on one large holder's goodwill. Weitsman's holdings in CDB were not quantified (see the on-chain collector workstream).

## 7. Competitive threats and displacement

- [I] **Fungible displacement is already visible.** The Base DICKBUTT memecoin (CoinMarketCap snapshot: about 489k holders) is a far larger "Dickbutt" community than the NFT's 2.1k owners. Meme attention can be monetised without the NFT.
- [I] **CC0 derivatives** (DickToadz, Moonbutts and others) can dilute the brand freely.
- [I] **The original creator's disavowal** limits any official or licensed extension, such as merchandise or media.
- [I] **Market structure.** In a 6-sales-per-bar market, Blur's closure (2026-11-12) and the removal of Blend bids could widen spreads further.

## 8. "If the team vanished tomorrow, would collectors still want this in 2028?"

[I] **The team already vanished, in 2023, and collectors still hold it.** The owner count is stable at about 2.1k, and the collection rallied 8× in BTC terms in 2025 without a team. So by this criterion CDB is the least founder-dependent of the three.

The cost is that it has no one manufacturing demand. Its value then rests on:
- the durability of a 20-year-old meme;
- a few committed whales, which is now a concentration risk around Weitsman and PenusDAO;
- the OG status of CryptoPunks-adjacent 2021 culture.

It will plausibly still be wanted by a niche in 2028. Price is likely to remain hostage to ETH/BTC cycles and to thin-market whims.

## 10. Strongest bull and bear theses

**Bull [I]**
- A genuinely founder-independent, 20-year-old meme with CryptoPunks-OG lineage, CC0 openness and a small 5,200 supply.
- It survived a full team exit.
- Its 2025 behaviour shows high positive convexity to ETH/BTC upswings: BTC-floor went from 0.0078 (Apr 2025) to 0.0616 (Aug 2025).
- A committed whale (Weitsman, a stated never-seller) and a DAO hold the keys.
- Since 2025-01-01 it is roughly flat vs BTC (−0.1%), while Milady is −84% and Remilio −89%.

**Bear [I]**
- It held up only relative to the Remilia names, and only when measured from a late-2024 trough:
  - −51% vs BTC since Jan 2024.
  - −66% vs BTC over the last 12 months.
  - −94% from its BTC peak.
  - −41% vs BTC in 2026 YTD, the worst of the three this year.
- Liquidity is very thin (about 6 sales per 3-day bar), so floor marks may overstate realisable value.
- IP and provenance are contested: the original artist disavowed it, and it is CC0 yet "IP-acquired".
- The meme's mass audience has moved to a fungible Base memecoin.
- Governance is concentrated in one whale.
- No catalyst in its history has created lasting value.

**On "why it held up vs BTC in 2024–26" (evidence-limited) [I].** CDB's capitulation came early: −87% in ETH terms from Jul 2023 to Jul 2024, after the team exit. So it entered 2025 cheap, while Milady and Remilio peaked into the CULT airdrop (Dec 2024) and then unwound. CDB also had no token-airdrop overhang to unwind. Its 2025 rebound coincided with:
- the ETH/BTC recovery (0.018 to 0.040, Apr–Aug 2025);
- the DICKBUTT memecoin wave (Oct 2024 – Jan 2025);
- possibly the Weitsman/PenusDAO accumulation (date unverified).

No source documents the cause. Treat this as base effect plus beta, not demonstrated resilience.
